# A growing catalogue of public AI sources

## One catalogue, two kinds of record

The selected project's `data/publisher-catalogue.json` is the live curated catalogue. Read it before choosing recurring social/news sources. `data/sources.json` remains the acquisition authority and holds both individual story URLs and monitored entrypoints. The catalogue adds editorial purpose and dated assessments; it does not replace source results, evidence or candidate decisions.

The catalogue stays in the writable project so each research run can improve it without editing the installed skill. In a new project, create the same structure from sources actually inspected there; do not silently import this project's account assumptions. If the catalogue is missing in this project, reconstruct from its source ledger and saved public evidence, recording what remains unverified.

Keep one entry per recurring identity or publication. Different platforms may have separate entries linked as the same owner; do not count them as independent corroboration. Store individual posts/articles as evidence examples, not as catalogue identities or recurring monitors. Communities such as a subreddit are not individual social accounts.

## Discovery on every substantive research run

1. Revisit useful existing sources and the oldest unresolved discovery leads. Choose several seeds across different organizations, topics and editorial roles, including independent specialists where available. Start from demonstrated usefulness, not follower counts. Record the planned scope before exploration: normally 3–5 seeds, one interaction hop, a second hop only for a promising original author, and a bounded lead/time budget suited to the run. These are exploration defaults, not minimum additions; document a smaller or different scope and the reason.
2. Make a deliberate interaction-graph pass on public X or other relevant social pages. Inspect reposted and quoted accounts, authors receiving substantive replies, visible collaborators and credited original authors. Follow the actual public post or attribution that connects the seed to the candidate, not an inferred relationship. Preserve a source URL, edge type, observation date, available publication date and enough context to distinguish supportive, critical, mixed or unclear interactions. The edge is a discovery clue, never an endorsement or a reliability score. Do not automatically crawl follower/following graphs or expand every thread recursively.
3. Use at least one complementary discovery route, rotating across runs: author links from primary papers and project sites; GitHub maintainers/contributors; model-hub authors and organizations; conference/research listings; newsletter citations and interviews; curated public lists; or targeted public search for under-covered specialties and platforms. Public lists and aggregators are leads, not qualification. Trace promising work to its original author, and verify cross-platform identity through explicit public links rather than similar names or handles.
4. Inspect each promising identity's actual profile/feed/archive and representative relevant items. Check the profile's own identity/affiliation links against primary work where available; a badge or guessed handle is insufficient. Evaluate original contributions, technical care, practical usefulness, primary-artifact links and disclosed uncertainty. Prefer several dated examples over one viral post; state when only one item or a narrow slice was accessible. Separate an inspected profile from inspected posts and from full historical coverage. An inaccessible profile remains a deferred lead; do not invent content, quality or cadence from search snippets.
5. Actively add qualifying inspected identities to the catalogue and a distinct `monitor:true` source record if absent. Add new story URLs separately with `monitor:false`. Inspect a newly added monitor before marking it active; otherwise leave it unverified. An inspected article alone does not establish successful access to its publisher homepage. Preserve discovery provenance when a queued lead becomes an entry; an access wall warrants a concrete limitation and next action, not a fabricated verification.
6. Record actual seeds, routes, exploration bounds, inspected edges and considered identities, including additions, updates, rejections, unchanged entries and deferred leads with next actions. Zero additions is acceptable when no inspected candidate qualifies or access is blocked, with the attempted routes, evidence and concrete reasons recorded. Never fill a quota with low-value accounts, repeatedly defer every promising lead without inspection, or claim expansion merely because a plan was written.

A due-gate skip makes no research or catalogue-growth claim. Catalogue-only curation from saved evidence is labeled as such, not a newly completed scan or fresh source access.

## User-requested shared-follow discovery

When the user explicitly asks to find accounts followed by several existing candidates, treat this as a separate discovery mode. Choose a diverse, bounded seed set from the current catalogue, record its size and identity URLs, and inspect public **Following** lists. Preserve the dedicated run identity and news-scan watermarks. This does not enable unlimited graph expansion in scheduled runs.

Count an edge only when the candidate's own account card appears in the seed's Following list. Bio mentions, sidebar recommendations, “followers you know,” similar display names and a seed's follower count do not establish that relationship. Save the public list URL, observation time, seed and target identity, card locator and private capture hash. Canonicalize handles and count each seed once per target. Following is a discovery signal, not endorsement or independent corroboration.

Record pagination and coverage honestly. X may expose only an initial segment and a later segment after scrolling; a large visible Following total does not mean those accounts were all retrieved. Label intersections as **observed lower bounds within the sampled lists**, retain inaccessible or missing portions as unknown, and stop when an additional bounded navigation produces no new cards. Do not infer that an unseen account is not followed. Use counts to prioritize inspection, then apply the originality criteria below independently.

For a strict expansion request, inspect the actual profile, verify identity against explicit author/project links, and examine two distinct attributable substantive contributions, including relevant methods, results and limitations. Two posts describing the same experiment or launch do not establish two contributions. A current employer affiliation does not credit an individual with the employer's research; check the byline or contribution statement. Original technical explanation can qualify without claiming authorship of the explained research. Record commercial interests, occasional posting and older examples explicitly.

Keep the complete graph and raw captures in the ignored acquisition archive. Public account entries may show the observed seed count, seed handles and sample limitations using the directory's allowed fields. Report separately: seeds sampled, observed edges, unique targets, previously uncatalogued shared targets, candidates actually assessed, accepted identities and deferred identities. The unassessed pool is neither rejected nor vetted. Recompute final counts from the frozen graph after worker packets arrive so earlier partial counts do not survive integration.

## What qualifies

Prefer original announcements, reproducible work, useful implementation detail, careful comparisons, transparent limitations and links to primary artifacts. The owner wants an initial directory of at least 100 high-quality accounts; subsequent runs should expand it when evidence warrants. A requested count never turns unvisited leads, duplicate handles for the same identity, or repost-only aggregators into qualifying accounts. Normally inspect at least two distinct attributable contributions; one substantial original item can support a clearly limited specialist assessment, but a bio, affiliation, popularity, or multiple promotional posts about one item do not establish recurring originality. Separate an author’s own work from quoted work and record the observed basis for usefulness. Sample original posts as well as graph edges; following quotes alone can overselect aggregators. Preserve a role such as **official announcements**, **independent analysis**, **community discovery**, **specialist research** or **secondary tracker**. Vendor ownership is a conflict to disclose, not a reason to exclude a useful primary source.

Assess frequency only from observed dated posts/issues spanning a stated window. Several visible items can support “multiple relevant posts observed,” not a numeric daily cadence or “always first.” Follower count, verification badges and popularity do not establish accuracy. Older occasional essays can be valuable without being frequent news. Mark mixed-topic sources and narrow specialists accordingly.

Treat aggregator rankings, model launch trackers and repost accounts as leads. Verify dates, availability, licensing and performance at the origin. Do not mistake several syndications for independent confirmation. Retain low-yield or blocked entries with reasons rather than silently deleting history; reduce priority when warranted.

Reposting, quoting, replying or collaboration can surface useful specialists without making either account reliable. A substantive reply can challenge the seed's claim; quote context may be hostile, ironic or mixed. Assess the discovered account's own work independently, and record an unclear interaction as unclear. Never characterize an account as high quality, prolific or authoritative solely because a trusted seed interacted with it.

## Catalogue data and audit history

Use the schema documented in the project's `DATA_CONTRACT.md`. Preserve stable entry IDs and source-ledger IDs. At minimum, retain canonical identity URL, title, kind/platform, topics, role, assessment, observed examples, access limits and dated provenance. Keep retrieval date distinct from a post's publication date; unknown dates stay null. Unvisited profile links belong in `discoveryQueue` until inspected.

Add one `discoveryRuns` record per substantive run; append resumed work to that run without losing prior observations. Link to the real full-scan run ID when part of a news scan. For an explicitly requested standalone live profile-discovery run, use its own stable discovery ID, `runId:null`, `purpose:"standalone_live_profile_discovery"`, and actual start/finish times. Preserve the news-scan cadence and source-coverage watermarks; this work is not a completed news scan. Include concrete considered URLs, additions/updates/rejections/deferred leads, counts, actual search strategy and a reason if no entries were added. An initial migration may use a descriptive curation ID, with its type explicitly stating saved-evidence curation. Do not invent a research run or source-check timestamp.

Use additive discovery metadata within the project's schema: `discoveryRuns[].strategy` records seed profile URLs, routes actually attempted, depth/lead/time bounds and limitations; each `consideredItems[]` record retains `discoveryProvenance[]` with `{fromUrl,toUrl,sourceUrl,type,observedAt,publishedAt:null,context}`. `sourceUrl` is the public page on which the edge or attribution was actually observed; `type` distinguishes repost, quote, substantive reply, collaboration, original-author attribution, primary-author link, repository/model-hub author, paper author, newsletter citation, public list or search. Keep unknown post dates null and record search query/locator when relevant. Copy the relevant provenance onto an entry or queued lead so promotion does not erase its origin. Deferred items need a concrete next action. Multiple routes to the same identity preserve multiple observed links, while considered-identity counts remain deduplicated. Existing records may lack these new fields; never backfill fictional edges or dates.

Before saving, mechanically verify unique IDs/canonical URLs, public credential-free URLs, evidence references and counts. Compare each `sourceId` to an actual monitored ledger record; catalogue growth and source-ledger growth are separate counts. An entry's current assessment must agree with dated observations, not a stale free-text note.

Run the bundled read-only helper, `python3 <skill-directory>/scripts/validate_catalogue.py <project-directory>`. Add `--check-captures` when ignored local archives are available to check their hashes and evidence URL occurrence. This catches structural/accounting errors; it cannot establish that a passage supports an interpretation, that an account is reliable, or that a source's history was exhausted.

The original catalogue includes inspected public X profiles, subreddits, newsletters and independent blogs. It also includes concrete unvisited author/account links as leads. Consult the current file for names and latest evidence rather than copying a static list into this skill. Local provenance files can help an agent resume work, but publicly readable source URLs must support the assessment; never include private account information, raw traces or machine-specific absolute paths.

## Public account directory

Generate the website's account directory from the catalogue through the project's explicit public-field allowlist. Do not copy the raw file or hand-maintain a parallel list. Include profile links, platform, topics/editorial role, concrete usefulness, assessment status, observed examples with publication/retrieval dates distinguished, and access/coverage limits. Keep status language precise: `verified` means the recurring surface was inspected and relevance evidenced, not that every claim was validated. Narrow specialists and provisional, blocked or retired accounts must retain their honest labels; a community or publisher is not counted as an individual account.

Keep unvisited `discoveryQueue` links out of the curated account directory. If a future interface exposes leads separately, label them explicitly as unvisited/unverified and never count them among vetted accounts. Exclude ignored capture paths/hashes, local checkpoints, raw traces, private acquisition state and `.state/usage/` from public projections, reports, builds and commits. Public URLs and concise reviewed observations provide the useful evidence; the private acquisition archive is not a public asset. Directory generation or catalogue edits never modify source watermarks or imply a completed source scan.

## Review for accuracy, not forced growth

Before closing a run, sample a newly added identity and a rejected/deferred lead, where each category exists. Trace a recorded discovery edge to its public evidence and check that the interpretation preserves quote/reply context. Verify that evidence supports the claimed identity, topical relevance and cadence, and that a story has not accidentally become a recurring monitor. When there are no additions, review the actual effort and deferred next actions instead of assuming growth occurred. Recheck cited archive links when a prior note claims none exist: an extraction summary can omit links that are present in the saved DOM. Record corrections as dated observations. After a directory change, inspect the rendered list and an example account; verify profile/example links, status and date labels, limitations, counts and the absence of private fields.
